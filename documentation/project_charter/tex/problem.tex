The problem statement defines the "Why" of the project. This is the higher purpose, or the reason for the project's existence. This section should avoid mentioning implementation details, and focus more on what the current problem is and what would be gained if the problem were to be solved. In short, the is the reason that you are going to be working on something, not the method(s) that you will be employing.

In Today society, people have almost little to no knowledge or sometimes no care in their own IOT systems. Especially over the internet where
user information is always travelling and being stored whenever you access the web through your personal computer, data integrity, confidentially, and availability
is always the rise to concern. The question then begs to differ on, can this be maintained and monitored on a personal level. The goal of this SIEM design is to handle
such question. To have to ability to monitor your credentials and information like a real SIEM would and apply beneficial feedback. From this system society will be able to 
learn and understand a SIEM sytem composed with alert system design to help you understand and visualize any suspicious activities that occur in the background of your local system
