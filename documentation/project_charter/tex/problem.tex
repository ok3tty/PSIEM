In today’s society, many individuals have limited knowledge of, or show little concern for, the security of their own IoT systems. This issue is especially pressing on the internet, where user information is constantly transmitted and stored. Each time someone accesses the web through a personal device, concerns over data integrity, confidentiality, and availability inevitably arise.

This raises an important question: can such concerns be effectively maintained and monitored at a personal level? The goal of this SIEM design project is to explore that possibility. The system is intended to provide individuals with the ability to monitor their credentials and information in a manner similar to a professional SIEM, while also delivering meaningful feedback.

Through this design, users will gain insight into how a SIEM operates and how an integrated alert system can identify and visualize suspicious activities occurring within their local environment. Ultimately, this project seeks to help society better understand the importance of personal system monitoring and the role of SIEMs in safeguarding digital information.
